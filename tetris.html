Click “Show on-screen buttons” for touch controls: ◀ ▶ move, ⟳ rotate, ▼ soft drop, ⤓ hard drop. Hold ◀ ▶ ▼ to repeat.

Scoring

1 line = 100, 2 = 300, 3 = 500, 4 = 800, multiplied by your level. You level up every 10 lines and the pieces fall faster. Clearing lines triggers a 2-second effect (bigger for more lines) and the game freezes until it ends. The faint outline shows where the piece will land.
